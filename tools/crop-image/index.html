How to crop an image

  1. Choose your image. Drop a JPG, PNG or WebP onto the box above, or click Choose File.
  2. Pick a shape. Click a preset — 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 or 4:5 — or choose Free and draw any rectangle on the picture.
  3. Adjust. Drag the box to move it and pull the corners to resize it. The exact pixel size is shown as you go.
  4. Download. The cropped image is saved in its original format, at full quality.

Cropping happens in your browser, so your photo is never uploaded.

Crop without losing quality

Cropping only removes pixels from the edges; the part you keep is not re-scaled. JPG and WebP files are saved again at high quality (95), PNG files stay lossless, and GIFs are saved as PNG. To make the picture smaller in pixels afterwards, use Resize Image; to make the file smaller, use Reduce Image File Size.

Tips

  • Keep faces and important details away from the very edge — some apps round the corners or show a circle.
  • For a sharp result, crop from the original photo rather than from a screenshot of it.
  • Crop first, then compress: removing background makes the file smaller too.

Frequently asked questions

How do I crop a photo to a square?

Open the photo, click the 1:1 preset, move the square over the part you want and download.

Does cropping reduce image quality?

No. The area you keep stays at its original resolution. Only the edges you cut away are removed.

Can I crop to an exact pixel size?

Use a preset or Free mode to choose the area, and watch the pixel size shown under the presets. To end up at exact dimensions, such as 600 × 600, crop to the right shape here and then use Resize Image.

Which formats can I crop?

JPG, PNG, WebP and GIF. JPG, PNG and WebP are downloaded in the same format; a GIF is saved as a PNG (only its first frame).

Is my photo uploaded?

No. Cropping happens in your browser and the image never leaves your device.
