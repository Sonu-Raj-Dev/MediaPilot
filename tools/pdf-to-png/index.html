Frequently Asked Questions

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. MediaPilot turns the pages into PNG images entirely in your browser. Your PDF never leaves your device.

Which quality should I choose?

Medium (150 DPI) looks sharp on any screen. Choose High (300 DPI) if you will print the images, or Low (72 DPI) for the smallest files.

Why are PNG files bigger than JPG?

PNG is lossless: it keeps every pixel exactly, so text and diagrams stay perfectly sharp. That precision makes the files larger than JPG.

Can I get JPG images instead?

Yes — use the PDF to JPG converter.
